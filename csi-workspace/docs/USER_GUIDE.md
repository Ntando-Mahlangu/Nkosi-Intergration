# CSI Workspace — User Guide

## Signing in

Use your CSI email and password. If Google Workspace sign-in is enabled, choose **Continue with Google Workspace**. If an administrator created your account, you will be asked to choose a new password at first sign-in.

## My CSI Workspace (home)

The home page answers *what do I need to do?*:

- **Open tasks / Due today / Awaiting review**: counts are calculated from your actual tasks. Click a card to open that filtered list.
- **Needs your confirmation**: actions suggested to you from meetings. They are **not** your responsibility until you accept them.
- **My Outstanding Actions**: tick the box to complete a task. Tasks that need review are submitted to the reviewer instead of being completed.
- **My Active Projects**, **Upcoming meetings** (with preparation notes and join links), **Recent activity** and **Quick actions**.

Press `/` anywhere to jump to search. The **New** button creates tasks, meeting notes, knowledge items, opportunities and learnings.

## Tasks

- The views are Today, This week, Overdue, Upcoming, Awaiting review, Completed, Meeting actions, Project actions, Personal and To review.
- **Personal** tasks (no project) are private to you. Nobody else sees them, including management and administrators.
- **Review workflow:** finish the work, then choose **Submit for review**. The reviewer approves it or requests changes with feedback, and requested changes return the task to you.
- **Blocked:** setting a task to Blocked requires a reason. Dependencies show which tasks must finish first.
- **Export:** the Export CSV link exports your current list.

## Meetings

1. **Record a meeting.** Use **New → Submit meeting notes**. You can fill in the structured form, or paste or upload notes and transcripts (.txt, .md, .vtt, .srt). Imported transcripts are kept separate from the meeting record.
2. **Extract actions & decisions.** This proposes a summary, decisions, actions, risks, a project update and knowledge items, each with the source text it came from.
   - With AI configured, Claude writes the proposals. Otherwise a rule-based extractor looks for markers such as `Action:`, `Decision:`, `Risk:` and "*Name* to …".
   - Every proposal is labelled with which method produced it.
3. **Review.** On the review screen, accept, edit or reject each suggestion, or select several at once. Nothing is assigned until you accept it.
   - Owners and deadlines appear only if the notes state them.
   - Accepted actions for other people reach them as suggestions they must confirm.
4. **Approve the record**, then **Generate call report**.

**Tip:** use the *Meeting notes template* in the Knowledge Hub. Notes written with clear markers give much better rule-based extraction.

## Call reports and other reports

Reports open as a branded document. Use **Edit** while the report is a draft; every saved edit creates a new version.

**Workflow:** Submit for review → Approve or Request changes → Finalise or Publish → Archive.

**Exports:**
- **PDF:** opens a print-ready page; choose *Save as PDF* in your browser's print dialog.
- **HTML** and **Markdown** downloads.

## Projects & Programmes

- **Portfolio:** filter by owner, team, programme, status, priority and health.
- **Project page tabs:** Overview (progress, with *How is this calculated?*), Tasks, Milestones, Deliverables, Risks & issues, Decisions, Updates, Meetings, Reports, Documents, Activity and Related.
- **Health** is set by the project owner. The system only *suggests* a health value and lists the factors behind it, such as overdue milestones, open blockers or a stale update.
- **Deliverables:** submit a link to your work. The reviewer approves or requests changes, and each submission is versioned.
- **Post a project update** via **New → Update a project**.

## Knowledge Hub

- Browse by category, see recently added and popular items, items for your projects, procedures and lessons learned.
- **Ask CSI Knowledge** answers from records you are allowed to see and cites them. Without AI it shows the most relevant records instead.
- **Contributing:** create a draft, then submit it for review. A knowledge manager publishes it.
- **Access levels:** all staff, project members, management, or named people.

## Reports & Fakawe

The **Fakawe** tab shows the current reporting period:
- the deadline
- which active projects have not posted an update
- how many source records the period has
- what needs review first.

Coordinators generate a draft from approved records in the period. The draft lists its sources and quality flags. It stays a draft until it is approved.

## Opportunities

- **Pipeline board:** drag cards between stages, or use each card's stage selector.
- **Opportunity record:** log engagements, create follow-up tasks, and link related projects, knowledge and meetings.
- **Suggested signals** come from approved meetings and published knowledge that mention interest. They are suggestions until you convert them.

## Learning & Improvement

- **Share a learning:** what you tried, what you learned, and what you would do differently. Every field is optional, and you never have to claim that something changed.
- **Private learnings** are visible only to you.
- **Monthly themes:** contribute reflections to the current theme.
- **Improvement register:** suggest improvements and follow their status.

## Notifications & profile

- **Bell icon:** opens the notification centre, where you can mark notifications read or archive them.
- **Profile:** edit your name and job title, change your password, and mute notification types you don't need.
