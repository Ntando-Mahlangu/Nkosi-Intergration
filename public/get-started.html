No new workflow for your team to learn — LeadRecovery runs alongside however you already take enquiries.

  • Connect your phone number, website form, or chat widget — or import a CSV of existing leads.

  • A reply that sounds ready to book lands in your needs-attention inbox for a real person to close.
  • Everything is logged — every message, every reply, every outcome — for your own reporting and records.
