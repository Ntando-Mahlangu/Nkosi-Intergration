Win-back messaging

Periodic check-ins to past customers — distinct from the recovery messaging every lead already gets, which never contacts someone once they've converted. Turning this on only ever messages a specific lead once you've recorded that they personally agreed to it (the "agreed to occasional win-back messages" checkbox on their own detail view in the list dashboard) — this toggle alone messages no one. See COMPLIANCE.md "Win-back messaging for past customers" before turning this on: most jurisdictions treat this as direct marketing to an existing customer, which commonly needs its own opt-in distinct from whatever let you message them the first time.

Data retention

How long after a lead is fully closed out (unqualified, fraudulent, or converted) before it's automatically deleted along with its message history. Leads still active in your pipeline are never auto-deleted regardless of age, and a lead marked do-not-contact/opted-out is never auto-deleted either — that record is what stops LeadRecovery from re-contacting them if they're ever imported again.
