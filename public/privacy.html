3. Cookies and local storage

The Service does not use cookies, and does not use any third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The dashboards (this page and the client/admin consoles) use your browser's localStorage — a single entry, holding the API base URL and session credential you connected with — solely so you stay signed in across page loads without re-entering it every time. This is strictly functional storage necessary to operate the Service you asked for, not tracking: nothing in it identifies you across sites, is shared with any third party, or is used for advertising. It never leaves your browser except back to this Service's own API, and clearing it (or using "disconnect"/"log out" in any dashboard) removes it immediately.

4. How we use this information

  • To operate the Service — compose, send, and track messages; classify replies; run the automated reply assistant when you enable it.
  • To notify your team when a lead reply needs human attention (via a webhook URL you configure, e.g. Slack).
  • To provide activity/ROI reporting back to you.
  • To maintain security (rate limiting, audit logging of admin actions) and to troubleshoot issues.
  • To bill you, where applicable, via our payment processor.

We do not sell lead data, and we do not use it to train any model beyond the specific automated-reply behavior you configure for your own account.

5. Third-party processors

Operating the Service means sharing the minimum necessary data with:

  • Twilio — to send/receive SMS and WhatsApp messages, when you configure that channel.
  • SendGrid — to send/receive email, when you configure that channel.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to compose automated reply answers from your own knowledge-base text, and optionally to help classify ambiguous inbound replies, only when you enable those features.
  • Paddle — to process billing, if your account is billed through it.
  • Our hosting/database provider — [name your host] — to store and run the Service.

Each of these processes data under its own privacy terms; we only send what each integration actually needs to function.

6. Data retention

We retain your account and lead data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal/accounting purposes unless you request deletion sooner. Deleting a tenant account permanently removes its leads, message history, and any team member accounts from our systems (this cascades immediately and cannot be undone). An internal record that an account existed and what administrative actions were taken on it (our own audit log) is kept independently of that deletion, for our own accountability — it records only an account identifier and the action taken, not your leads' data.

7. Security

Provider credentials (Twilio/SendGrid) are encrypted at rest. Administrative actions are logged to an audit trail. Access to your account is gated by a per-tenant API key; we recommend rotating it immediately if you suspect it has been exposed.

8. Your rights

You can export a full copy of your lead data at any time (in CSV or JSON) directly from the Service. You can request correction or deletion of your account and its data by contacting us, or by asking us to delete your tenant, which removes it immediately. 9. Leads' own rights

A lead who no longer wants to be contacted can reply STOP (SMS/ WhatsApp) or an equivalent opt-out phrase, which the Service recognizes automatically and suppresses further outreach to that contact. As the business client, you are responsible for handling any further data-subject request a lead makes directly to you.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this Service.
